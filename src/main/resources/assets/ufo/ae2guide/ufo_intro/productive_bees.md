# Productive Bees

When [Productive Bees](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/productive-bees) is
installed, UFO Future adds three custom bees that passively regenerate the two bulk
resources the pack consumes by the thousands: matter balls and the scrap chain.

They are deliberately strong — maximum productivity and weather tolerance genes — and
deliberately hard to get: **the only way to obtain one is the Quantum Pattern
Fabrication Matrix**.

## The Bees

| Bee | Flower | Honeycomb centrifuges into |
|-----|--------|---------------------------|
| Matter Ball Bee | <ItemLink id="ae2:matter_ball" /> | 32–64 <ItemLink id="ae2:matter_ball" /> |
| Scrap Bee | <ItemLink id="ufo:scrap" /> | 64–128 <ItemLink id="ufo:scrap" /> |
| Scrap Box Bee | <ItemLink id="ufo:scrap_box" /> | 4–8 <ItemLink id="ufo:scrap_box" /> |

Each comb also centrifuges into wax as usual. Process the combs in the
[centrifuge](https://productive-bees.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) and let the bees work
inside an advanced hive.

## Obtaining Bees — QMF only

The bees cannot be found in the world, hatched from nests or bred into existence.
Every bee is fabricated by the QMF as a configurable spawn egg:

- **Matter Ball Bee** — matter balls, obsidian matrix, dimensional processors,
  quantum anomalies and liquid starlight.
- **Scrap Bee** — scrap, obsidian matrix, dimensional processors, quantum anomalies
  and water.
- **Scrap Box Bee** — scrap boxes, obsidian matrix, dimensional processors, quantum
  anomalies and water.

All three run on the QMF at MK2; check JEI for the exact amounts, energy and time.
`selfbreed` is disabled, so filling an advanced hive bank means running the QMF recipe
again for each additional bee — a permanent passive production line is an investment.

## Notes

- Everything above is gated on Productive Bees being present; without the mod, no bee
  content is loaded and the QMF recipes simply do not exist.
- The bees do not die of old age. Protect them like the machines that made them.
