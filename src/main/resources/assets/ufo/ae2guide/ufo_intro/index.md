# UFO Future

**UFO Future** extends Applied Energistics 2 from advanced matter synthesis to
parallel quantum factories, astronomical storage and stellar simulation.

![UFO Future](../pic/logo.png)

This guide describes the behavior implemented by the current 1.21.1 build. Use
JEI for exact pack-specific recipes; datapacks and server configuration may
change costs while the machine workflow remains the same.

## Start Here

- [Getting Started](getting_started.md) — the shortest path from AE2 to a
  working DMA and your first universal multiblock.
- [Machines](machines.md) — DMA, quantum production line and Stellar Nexus.
- [Infrastructure](infrastructure.md) — tiers, field generators, hatches,
  crafting storage and energy cells.
- [Materials and Storage](materials.md) — catalysts, stellar matter,
  containment and high-capacity cells.
- [Equipment](equipment.md) — Structure Scanner, energy tools and armor sets.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) — formation, grid, thermal and
  autocrafting diagnostics.

## Progression at a Glance

1. Establish a powered AE2 network and produce the first DMA components.
2. Use the <ItemLink id="ufo:dimensional_matter_assembler" /> for flexible
   item/fluid recipes, catalysts and early stellar matter.
3. Build the universal multiblock line and connect a
   <ItemLink id="ufo:quantum_pattern_hatch" /> for AE2 processing patterns.
4. Upgrade Stellar Field Generators from MK1 to MK3 to unlock recipes and
   accelerate lower-tier work.
5. Expand crafting CPUs and storage for extreme requests.
6. Build the <ItemLink id="ufo:stellar_nexus_controller" /> for final-tier
   simulations and long-cycle production.

> The Entropic Assembler Matrix is intentionally not documented as a production
> machine in this edition because its implementation is scheduled for a complete
> redesign.
