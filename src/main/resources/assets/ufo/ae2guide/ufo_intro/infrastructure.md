# Infrastructure

This section covers the blocks that make UFO machines part of an AE2 factory:
field tiers, pattern dispatch, directional hatches, large crafting CPUs and
energy buffers.

<SubPages />

## Reliable Factory Checklist

1. Connect each directional hatch on the face shown by its block orientation.
2. Keep the controller and hatches on the same powered grid.
3. Provide crafting storage large enough for the complete request.
4. Leave storage space for promised outputs and probabilistic byproducts.
5. Upgrade every required field position; one lower field can reduce the whole
   universal machine's tier.
