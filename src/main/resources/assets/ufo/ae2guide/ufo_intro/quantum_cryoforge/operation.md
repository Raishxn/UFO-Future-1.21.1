# Cryoforge Coolant Production

The Cryoforge produces the thermal fluids used by universal multiblocks and the
Stellar Nexus. Its recipe tier is derived from the uniform Stellar Field
Generator tier installed in the structure.

## Progression

- Establish the early Gelid Cryotheum route first.
- Upgrade every field position together; a single lower tier prevents the
  intended higher machine tier.
- Stable Coolant is a **MK3 Cryoforge** recipe.
- Use the produced high-density coolant to stabilize larger parallel workloads.

## Port Planning

Flexible casing positions accept the universal hatch family. Plan item input,
fluid input/output and AE energy access before filling the shell. Massive hatches
connect through their indicated face, so orient external ME cabling deliberately.

The machine shares the 27-thread persistent process model. A full destination
does not void coolant output; the corresponding process remains blocked until
capacity returns.
