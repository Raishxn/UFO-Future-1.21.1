# Armor Sets

UFO Future has two armor stages: the **Thermal Resistor Exosuit** and the modular **UFO Armor**.

## Thermal Resistor Exosuit

The mask, chest, pants and boots are made through DMA recipes. A full set protects against the DMA's hazard-zone heat and the hazardous-material inventory effects. A full UFO Armor set also provides this protection; individual pieces are insufficient.

Start with Thermal Resistor Plating, then check the current piece recipes in JEI/EMI. The plating recipe uses Netherite, Obsidian Matrix, Blue Ice and water. The older wiki's Gelid Cryotheum input for plating is no longer correct.

## Modular UFO Armor

The four UFO Armor pieces are **QMF recipes**. Their abilities come from installed cards, so equipping a bare set does not automatically grant flight, night vision or invulnerability. Effects require the full set, an enabled module in its designated armor piece and enough stored RF for that module.

| Piece | Module slots | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Helmet | 5 | Abyssal Sight, Adaptive Biosphere, Quantum Relay |
| Chestplate | 8 | Aegis Singularity, Reality Anchor, Void Flight, Chrono Regenerator |
| Leggings | 5 | Entropy Magnet, Matter Translocator, Singularity Strike |
| Boots | 4 | Phase Step, Kinetic Overdrive |

There are **16 module types**. Open the armor configuration interface to install, enable and adjust them; the server may cap configurable values. Examples:

- **Void Flight** grants flight while the full set is worn and the chestplate has energy.
- **Abyssal Sight** supplies night vision and counters darkness.
- **Aegis Singularity** can cancel incoming damage by spending energy.
- **Reality Anchor** can rescue the wearer from the void or a fatal event by spending energy.
- **Adaptive Biosphere** supplies fire resistance and water breathing.

Use JEI/EMI for current armor and card recipes. The previous fixed DMA recipe and fixed 400 RF/s claim for the UFO set described an older design.
