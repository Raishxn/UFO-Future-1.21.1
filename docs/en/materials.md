# Materials & Fluids

The current 3.0 recipes use a progression from White Dwarf through Neutron Star and Pulsar fragments. Those materials feed processor parts, component matrices and the matter chain.

## Matter chain

Proto Matter → Corporeal Matter → White Dwarf / Neutron Star / Pulsar Matter → Dark Matter. The DMA starts the chain; the QMF handles large batches later.

## Coolant and recipe fluids

Recipe fluid and coolant are separate inputs. Gelid Cryotheum is the early coolant; Stable Coolant and Temporal Fluid support higher loads. The Quantum Cryoforge produces Stable Coolant and other cryogenic outputs. Feed multiblock coolant externally through the ME Massive Fluid Hatch.
