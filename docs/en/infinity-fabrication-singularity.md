# Infinity Fabrication Singularity

The **Infinity Fabrication Singularity** is the endgame aggregate crafter. It replaces the older Quantum Crafter role with persistent production routes.

## Production

- Supports up to **128 persistent pattern routes**.
- Enabled routes repeat automatically; large requests execute all available copies through the same aggregate executor.
- Connect its outward-facing **Quantum Grid Link** to the ME network.
- Supply its **FE Energy Input Hatch** externally. The energy hatch alone does not connect the machine to ME.

Use the controller to manage enabled patterns and the structure preview to locate the Grid Link and energy hatch.
