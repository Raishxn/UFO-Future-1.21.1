# Tools & Weapons

UFO Future's energy tool changes between **Staff, Sword, Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Hammer, Greatsword, Fishing Rod and Bow**. Stored energy persists when switching form. The Hammer supports area mining; the Pickaxe and Hammer can auto-smelt.

## Structure Scanner

The **Structure Scanner** now has more than a Creative quick-build action:

1. **Right-click in the air** to open its settings. Choose **Scan, Build, Replace or Demolish**, hatch placement, and MK1/MK2/MK3 field tier.
2. **Right-click a supported controller** in Scan mode to validate its structure. The scanner reports missing or misplaced blocks and highlights their positions.
3. In a construction mode, **sneak and right-click the controller** to start the chosen action. Build and Replace use real materials; the scanner can return replaced blocks exactly.
4. To use AE2 storage as a material source, enable the network option and right-click an **AE2 Wireless Access Point** with the scanner to link it. The access point and player must be in the same loaded dimension.

The in-game structure preview remains useful for finding hatch and field positions before building.
